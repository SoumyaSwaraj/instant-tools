    What is an image converter?

    An image converter re-encodes a picture from one file format into another, such as WebP to JPG or PNG to WebP. JPG is best for photos, PNG is lossless and keeps transparency, and WebP offers both smaller files and transparency.

    This converter works entirely in your browser: each file is decoded by the browser, drawn onto an HTML canvas and exported with canvas.toBlob in the format you pick. It reads any format your browser can display — JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF (first frame), BMP, SVG and AVIF on supported browsers. Because JPG has no transparency, transparent pixels are filled with the background color you choose (white by default). Nothing is uploaded, and it works offline.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the image converter free and safe?

    Yes. It is free with no limits, and your images never leave your device because conversion happens locally in your browser.

    How do I convert WebP to JPG?

    Drop the WebP file onto the tool, choose JPG as the output format and click Download. You can convert many WebP files at once.

    What happens to transparency when converting PNG to JPG?

    JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas are filled with a background color. White is the default, and you can pick any color.

    Which format should I choose?

    Use JPG for photos and maximum compatibility, PNG for screenshots, logos and lossless quality, and WebP for the smallest files on the web.

    Does converting reduce image quality?

    Converting to PNG is lossless. JPG and WebP are lossy; a quality of 90 keeps results visually identical to the original for most images.
