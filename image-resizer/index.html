How to resize an image

  1. Drop an image onto the resizer, or tap to choose one.
  2. Enter a new width and height in pixels, a percentage, or tap a preset such as 1080×1080.
  3. Choose the output format and click Download resized image.

What is an image resizer?

An image resizer changes the pixel dimensions of a picture — for example from 4000×3000 to 1920×1440. Smaller dimensions mean smaller files and faster pages, while exact sizes are required by social networks: 1080×1080 for Instagram posts, 1920×1080 for Full HD and 1200×630 for link previews.

This resizer runs entirely in your browser. The image is drawn onto an HTML canvas at the new size using high-quality smoothing and exported as JPG, PNG or WebP. When the target aspect ratio differs from the original you can crop to fill (no distortion), fit inside with padding, or stretch. No file is uploaded, there is no size limit beyond your device memory, and it works offline.

Frequently asked questions

How do I resize an image without losing quality?

Keep the aspect ratio locked and only scale down. Downscaling with high-quality smoothing stays sharp; enlarging beyond the original size always adds blur because new pixels are interpolated.

How do I resize a photo to 1080×1080 for Instagram?

Drop the photo, tap the 1080×1080 preset and keep Crop to fill selected. The image is scaled and center-cropped to a perfect square without distortion.

Can I resize by percentage?

Yes. Switch to Percent mode and enter a value such as 50% to halve both width and height while keeping the proportions.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Resizing happens locally in your browser with the Canvas API. Your images never leave your device and the tool works offline.

Which output format should I pick?

Keep the original format for most uses. Choose JPG for photos, PNG for graphics with transparency and WebP for the smallest web-ready files.
