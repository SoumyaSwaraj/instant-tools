How to crop an image

  1. Drop an image onto the cropper, or tap to choose one.
  2. Drag the crop box and its handles, pick an aspect ratio such as 1:1 or 16:9, or type exact X, Y, width and height values.
  3. Choose a format and click Crop & download to save the cropped image at full resolution.

What is an image cropper?

An image cropper removes the outer parts of a picture so only a chosen rectangle remains. Cropping is used to improve composition, remove distractions and fit fixed aspect ratios such as 1:1 for profile pictures, 16:9 for YouTube thumbnails and 9:16 for Stories and Reels.

This cropper runs entirely in your browser. The crop box is positioned in the image’s real pixel coordinates, so the exported file is cut from the original full-resolution image — never from the scaled-down preview. The selected area is drawn onto an HTML canvas and saved as JPG, PNG or WebP. It works with mouse, touch and keyboard (arrow keys move the box), nothing is uploaded, and it works offline.

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping reduce image quality?

No. The crop is taken from the original full-resolution image, so the pixels you keep are unchanged. Saving as PNG is lossless; JPG and WebP are saved at high quality.

How do I crop an image to a square?

Choose the 1:1 aspect preset. The crop box locks to a square that you can move and resize, then click Crop & download.

Can I enter exact crop dimensions?

Yes. Type X, Y, width and height in pixels in the number fields. Values are measured in the original image’s pixels and update live as you drag.

Does the cropper work on phones?

Yes. The crop box uses pointer events, so you can drag and resize it with your finger on Android and iPhone, and the page works offline once loaded.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Cropping happens locally in your browser with the Canvas API, so your photos never leave your device.
