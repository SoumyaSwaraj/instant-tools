How to rotate or flip an image

  1. Drop an image onto the tool, or tap to choose one.
  2. Click Rotate left, Rotate right or 180°, drag the angle slider for a custom rotation, and use Flip horizontal or Flip vertical.
  3. Choose an output format and click Download to save the image at full resolution.

What is Rotate & Flip Image?

Rotate & Flip Image is a free browser tool that changes an image’s orientation. Rotating by 90°, 180° or 270° is lossless in geometry — every pixel is kept and width and height simply swap for 90° and 270°. Flipping horizontally creates a mirror image, which is useful for selfies and scanned documents.

Custom angles such as 2.5° straighten tilted horizons; the canvas expands to fit the rotated image so no corner is cut off, and the empty corners are transparent (PNG/WebP) or filled with your background color (JPG). Processing uses the HTML Canvas API at the original resolution, so nothing is uploaded and the tool works offline. Very large images are limited by your browser’s maximum canvas size (about 16,384 px per side on most devices).

Frequently asked questions

How do I rotate an image 90 degrees?

Drop the image and click Rotate right for 90° clockwise or Rotate left for 90° counter-clockwise, then click Download.

How do I mirror or flip a photo?

Click Flip horizontal to mirror the photo left-to-right, or Flip vertical to turn it upside down like a reflection. You can combine flips with any rotation.

Can I rotate by a custom angle?

Yes. Drag the angle slider or type any value between −180° and 180°, including decimals such as 1.5° to straighten a tilted horizon.

Does rotating reduce image quality?

The image is exported at full resolution. Rotations by multiples of 90° keep every pixel; custom angles resample the image with high-quality smoothing. Save as PNG for lossless output.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Rotation and flipping happen locally in your browser using the Canvas API, so your photos never leave your device and the tool works offline.
